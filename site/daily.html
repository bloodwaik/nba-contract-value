Every player earns the same paycheck every night (their annual salary divided by 82). But their on-court value swings game to game. We turn each night's Game Score into a dollar value using the night's own median as the benchmark, then compare to what the player was paid for that one game. The gap is single-game surplus — who earned their keep, who didn't.

How "$ earned" is computed

Game Score (Hollinger's box-score impact metric) is the closest free per-game number to EPM. For each slate we compute the median GmSc among players who played 20+ minutes and the median per-game salary among the same group. The ratio gives us a slate-specific $/GmSc rate. Multiply each player's GmSc by that rate to get "$ earned." Subtract their actual pro-rated salary to get nightly surplus.
